Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tarot App ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

The Tarot App is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with external servers or third parties.

All data generated within the App is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Saved tarot readings and personal interpretations
  • Notes and journal entries you create
  • App settings and language preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

2. Data Storage

All user-generated content (readings, notes, interpretations) is stored using a local SQLite database on your device. Language and appearance preferences are stored via your device's local storage (AsyncStorage). No cloud synchronisation or remote backup occurs unless you explicitly perform one yourself through your device's own backup tools.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services. No SDKs that collect personal data are included.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage / Local Database – to save your readings and notes locally.
  • System locale / Language – to detect your preferred language on first launch.

No other permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.) are requested or used.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for all ages in this regard.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We encourage you to use your device's built-in security features to protect your data.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. This permanently removes all locally stored readings, notes, and settings. Within the App, individual readings and notes can be deleted from the respective screens.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
